Send parcels across Kenya with Mzigo.online

Mzigo.online connects you to trusted courier partners across Kenya. Register a parcel online, drop it off at a partner office and track it all the way to collection — while the receiver pays the delivery fee by M-Pesa. We are also the first Kenyan company to offer parcel insurance, so every parcel can travel with real cover and peace of mind.

How Mzigo works

Three steps from your screen to the receiver's hands.

Step 1

Choose your courier and route

Compare partner offices, routes and delivery options for your parcel, then pick the one that fits your budget.

Step 2

Register and drop off

Add sender and receiver details, declare the parcel value and print your QR label. Hand the parcel in at the partner office.

Step 3

Track it to collection

Every scan appears on the tracking page. The receiver pays the delivery fee by M-Pesa at collection and gets a collection code.
