About Mzigo.online

Mzigo.online is a Kenyan parcel delivery platform connecting businesses and individuals with trusted courier partners — compare routes, register parcels, track scans and pay on collection by M-Pesa.

Kenya's first parcel delivery with insurance — and the peace of mind that comes with it

We built Mzigo.online around a simple promise: sending a parcel should never feel like a gamble. Parcel insurance is part of the platform, not an afterthought — declare what the parcel is worth, and if something goes wrong our claims process assesses approved payouts against that declared value.

  • Cover you can see: the declared value and cover are shown before you register.
  • Peace of mind: every scanned parcel travels with a defined claims route if the unexpected happens.
  • Clear process: open a claim online with the tracking number, description and estimated value.

Our mission

Make sending a parcel in Kenya simple, transparent and trackable — whether you are shipping one gift or hundreds of e-commerce orders a month.

What we do

We provide the technology layer: delivery quotes, parcel insurance, QR labels, tracking, M-Pesa collection and workspace tools that sit on top of established courier partner networks.

Who we serve

Growing businesses, market traders, busy families and freelancers running errands — plus the receivers who collect parcels across the country.

Our story

Mzigo.online started with a frustration every Kenyan sender knows: comparing couriers meant phone calls, WhatsApp messages and a trip to the office, and once the parcel left your hands, visibility disappeared.

We built one place to compare partner routes, register a parcel with a QR label, follow every scan and let the receiver pay on collection by M-Pesa. Then we went further — because "we'll look into it" is not a plan when a parcel is lost or damaged. That is why Mzigo.online became the first Kenyan company to offer parcel insurance, with cover tied to the value you declare and a claims process you can actually follow.

Today the platform supports individual senders, e-commerce stores and teams that need records, roles and an API — while keeping the sender, the receiver and the courier partner on the same page.

How the network works
  1. You register the parcel with the sender, receiver, size and declared value.
  2. You choose a partner and route from the courier options available for that destination.
  3. You drop off at a partner office and the parcel is scanned into the network.
  4. Everyone tracks every step — drop-off, transit, arrival and collection — while the receiver pays at collection by M-Pesa unless your workspace has agreed different terms.
