Where do I find my tracking number?

  • It is printed on the QR label or receipt given at drop-off.
  • You also receive it by SMS or WhatsApp when the parcel is registered.
  • Workspace users can copy it from Orders.
  • The sender can share the tracking link directly with the receiver.

What each scan means

  • Dropped off — the partner office has accepted the parcel.
  • In transit — the parcel is moving between partner offices.
  • Ready for collection — the parcel is at the destination office.
  • Collected — the receiver has paid (where applicable) and collected.

No scans yet?

Allow a little time for the next office to scan the parcel, then refresh. If a parcel is delayed, lost or damaged, open a claim from the Claims page or contact support.
