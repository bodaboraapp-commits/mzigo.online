## 22. Runner (freelancer) terms

  
These runner terms apply in addition to the rest of these Terms when you join Mzigo.online as a freelancer or runner. By creating a runner account, accepting a job or switching availability on, you agree to them.

  
### 22.1 Independent contractor

  
Runners are independent contractors, not employees, agents or representatives of Mzigo.online. You choose when to be available and which offered errands to accept. You are responsible for your own taxes, licences, insurance and equipment, and you may not hold yourself out as an employee of Mzigo.online.

  
### 22.2 Eligibility and account security

  
You must be at least 18 years old, provide accurate name, phone, service-area and payout details, and complete any identity verification we require. You are responsible for keeping your PIN secure and for all activity under your runner account. We may pause or decline accounts that fail verification or pose a safety or fraud risk.

  
### 22.3 Jobs and acceptance

  
Available errands are offered based on your service areas and availability. The fee for an errand is shown before you accept it, and you may decline jobs without penalty. Accepting a job creates a commitment to complete it with reasonable care and within the stated handling expectations. We do not guarantee a minimum number of jobs or earnings.

  
### 22.4 Conduct, safety and prohibited items

  
You must follow pickup, handling and delivery instructions, treat senders and receivers respectfully, and comply with all applicable laws. You may not accept errands involving illegal, dangerous, prohibited or misdescribed items, and you must report safety incidents, accidents or suspected fraud promptly through the support channels.

  
### 22.5 Payments and payouts

  
Completed jobs are recorded in your runner dashboard with the applicable fee. Pending earnings become available for payout subject to the minimum payout amount and any deductions disclosed in the app. Payouts are sent to the M-Pesa number saved in your runner profile. You are responsible for keeping that number accurate; payments sent to an incorrect number you supplied may not be recoverable. Mzigo.online records a payout reference for each payment.

  
### 22.6 Cancellation and no-show

  
If you cannot complete an accepted job, cancel as early as possible and follow the in-app process. Repeated late cancellations, no-shows or failure to mark deliveries accurately may lead to lower ratings, suspension or removal from the platform.

  
### 22.7 Insurance and liability

  
Parcel insurance offered through Mzigo.online covers parcels in accordance with the applicable terms; it does not provide personal accident, health, vehicle or income protection for runners. You are responsible for your own insurance. Nothing in these runner terms limits liability that cannot be limited under Kenyan law.

  
### 22.8 Suspension and termination

  
We may suspend or terminate runner access for breach of these terms, safety concerns, fraud, repeated quality failures or legal requirements. You may stop using the platform at any time. Accrued fees for valid completed jobs remain payable subject to these terms.

  
### 22.9 Changes

  
We may update these runner terms as the runner programme develops. Material changes will be notified through the app or by SMS/email before they take effect. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated runner terms.
