Operator help

Need a hand?

Quick scanner guidance. You can stay in the app while you work.

How do I scan a parcel?

Choose Scan below, tap Scan parcel label, and hold the QR or Locker label steady in the camera. The scanner stays on until a valid Mzigo label is detected.

What does each scan action mean?

Drop off records a parcel at the origin. Hand over gives it to the transporter. Arrival checks it in at the destination. Collect releases it to the customer.

The label will not scan

Type the tracking number in the manual entry field and tap the journey lookup. If manual entry is disabled, clean the label or ask your supervisor to enable manual entry for this point.

What happens when I am offline?

Scans are saved on this phone and show as queued in Recent. Keep the app open and reconnect; they sync automatically.

Why do I need a collection code?

Collection requires a completed payment and the customer's six-character code. Ask the customer to read it from their portal.

Wrong point or changed phone?

Open the gear in the header and use Disconnect point / change token. The point token is stored only on this device.
