Parcel sizes and what fits

Envelopes are ideal for documents; Small and Medium suit everyday boxes; Large and above cover bulky items. If your item is fragile, high-value or unusually shaped, declare it accurately and contact support before drop-off.

Paying on collection

The receiver pays the delivery fee at collection by M-Pesa and receives a collection code by SMS or WhatsApp.

Parcel insurance and peace of mind

Mzigo.online is the first Kenyan company to offer parcel insurance. Declare the parcel's value at registration to set your cover, then send knowing that a clear claims process stands behind the parcel.

What you need before you start
  • Sender and receiver names and phone numbers
  • Parcel description, size and declared value
  • Drop-off partner office that suits your route
